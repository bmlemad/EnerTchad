# Direct enquiry-form access

Typed investor, supplier and customer enquiry links preselected a subject but landed above the contact form. French and English links with a known profile now open the named, focusable form through #ctForm. General contact links and existing section targets are preserved.

The form uses its visible heading as its accessible name and has scroll clearance for fixed navigation. Explicit form arrivals receive focus without another scroll; language switching preserves the form destination. The change stores no entered personal fields and does not submit a message.

Regression checks cover direct and absolute links, query parameters, existing fragments, unrelated destinations, accessible form semantics, idempotence, language context and form focus. Existing production, route, language, download, search, metadata and performance checks remain enabled.
