01 · The company in brief

The ready-to-quote paragraph.

Use it verbatim — this is the official boilerplate, kept up to date here.

EnerTchad S.A. is an integrated oil company incorporated under OHADA law, currently in formation, with Chadian capital and talent. Organised around three core poles — Upstream, Midstream, Downstream — Petrochemicals extending Downstream — with four built-in capabilities, it aims to build and keep Chad’s entire hydrocarbon value chain in the country, “from source rock to pump”. Targeted capital trajectory: FCFA 10M → 1bn → 20bn. Dated objectives; no producing assets at this stage.

Company in formation: every figure and capability on this site is a dated objective, not an operating asset. Please keep that precision in your copy.
