EnerTchad · in brief

What grounds the ambition.

A company under formation: dated objectives, not producing assets — a ramp-up designed to build and keep value in-country, at every link of the chain.

OHADAgovernance · EITI targeted
The base

Regional governance standard + EITI transparency targeted from structuring onward.

100 %Chadian · investment thesis
The thesis

A market to win back, end-to-end vertical integration and a sequenced capital trajectory.

E-S-Gevery barrel, a smaller footprint
The bedrock

Environment, social, governance: more local content and the rigour of standards as the foundation of trust.

“Our natural resources must serve as a lever of growth and development.”

Giving purchasing power back to Chadians with the cheapest fuels, supporting the State’s energy sovereignty, and training the next generation before extracting: that is the EnerTchad Way.
