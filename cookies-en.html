Cookie policy

No tracking cookies · no third parties

EnerTchad does not use tracking cookies, nor advertising or social-media cookies. A short informative notice is shown on the first visit; no consent is required, as the site sets no non-essential cookies.

What we use

The site may keep, only in your browser (local storage), small display preferences to improve your reading comfort. This information stays on your device, is not personal and is never transmitted to a third party.

ItemPurposeDurationType
Theme preference / display settingsRemembering your appearance choiceUntil you clear itLocal storage — essential

What we do not use

No audience-analytics tool (neither Google Analytics nor equivalent), no advertising pixel, no tracking social button, no third-party cookie. Visual effects (animations, cursor highlight) store nothing.

Managing your preferences

You can clear this local storage at any time via your browser settings (“Clear site data”). No essential feature depends on it.
