On the ground

Our field commitment, in five practices

Principles are not enough: around every site, community engagement plays out in precise, repeatable practices — verifiable by residents themselves.

Practice 1

Listen before deciding

Before any siting: consultation with customary authorities, women and young people — separately where needed. The project adjusts to what it hears, and says so.

Practice 2

Put commitments in writing

Local jobs, water access, worksite hours: what is promised to a community is written, signed and posted — a spoken commitment is forgotten, a written one can be claimed.

Practice 3

Recruit as locally as possible

At equal skill, priority goes to people from the area; and where the skill is missing, we train it locally rather than importing it for good.

Practice 4

Measure and publish

Actual local jobs, purchases from local businesses, social projects delivered: the figures are published at regular intervals — residents judge on evidence.

Practice 5

Handle every grievance

An accessible register, a posted response time, follow-up through to closure: a complaint handled fast is a conflict avoided — and the record of grievances feeds the standards.

Territory · Land

The land before the derrick.

A wellpad, a pipeline, a depot cross cultivated fields. The IFC PS5 reference sets the order: avoid first, then compensate at a fair price — and no one should live worse after the works than before.

Avoid, then minimise

The route is discussed before the survey: consultation of rights-holders, alternatives studied, minimal footprint. Taking a field is the last resort, not the reflex — and temporary footprints are restored.

Compensate at a fair price

Crops, trees and improvements compensated at replacement cost, on a publicly agreed and displayed schedule — paid before entering the land. No works start on a promise.

Restore, not just pay

Livelihoods are followed over time: soil restoration, local hiring priority, post-works monitoring. Any disagreement goes through the grievance mechanism — four dated steps, no retaliation (community dialogue).

Target reference IFC PS5 · company in formation — commitments applicable from the first survey onwards.
