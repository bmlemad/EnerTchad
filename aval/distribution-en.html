Distribution: the link that touches every Chadian.

It is at the end of the chain — all the way to the pump — that the value of Chadian crude returns to the citizen. EnerTchad aims for a distribution model designed to give purchasing power back to Chadians by applying the approved price, with nothing added, guarantee access for the greatest number, and support the State in its energy-sovereignty policy.

Angle 01

Purchasing power & fair prices

Fuels at the approved price of each zone — including in the areas imports neglect. Margin control at every stage benefits the Chadian consumer first.

Angle 02

Hub-station network

Fewer stations, but better designed: hub stations with their own storage, designed to mesh the territory and secure supply all the way to remote areas.

Angle 03

Last mile & logistics

An integrated logistics cascade from local refining to the depot, then from depot to pump: each link feeds the next, down to the most isolated community.

Angle 04

Services & digital

The station becomes a place of services: e-shop, customer portal, and charging points EV charging to support tomorrow’s mobility.

Distribution cascade · from import to the pump

Each link feeds the next.

EnerTchad’s distribution is designed as an integrated cascade: at launch, the molecule enters through regional import; as local refining ramps up, it is produced in-country. In both cases value flows down to the last jerrycan, and each stage funds and secures the next. That integration is what makes a fair price possible, even far from the main roads.

Launch model · B2B

From commercial launch: import, store, deliver to professionals.

The modular mini-refinery is a dated target, not a day-one capability. The proposed launch model is professional finished-product supply, with contracted storage and delivery aligned with qualified demand. The three hubs remain a target network, to be developed progressively according to volumes, approvals and funding. Within downstream, this business would be the first stage; it would come after EnerTchad’s first project, obtaining an exploration and production licence. Its development would depend on contribution after delivery and customer collections, which may support subsequent stages — local refining, then the retail network.

Five source countries

Niger, Nigeria, Cameroon, Angola and Algeria. Two families of routes: overland regional haulage from Niger, Nigeria and Cameroon; and seaborne cargoes from Angola and Algeria, landed and then carried up the Douala–N’Djamena corridor. Diversifying origins means, first of all, depending on none.

Three storage hubs

N’Djamena commands the entry of the corridor and the first consumption basin; Moundou covers the oil, cotton and industrial south; Abéché opens the east. Storing near the customer rather than only at the point of entry shortens delivery times and absorbs supply shocks.

Four professional customer groups

Mining & public works, hauliers & fleets, agro-industry, standby power & institutions. Contracted volumes, fuel cards and on-site delivery — including the remote sites that today’s supply chains serve poorly.

Our method

From the depot to the pump: the journey in six steps

Every litre sold follows the same controlled path — and every step has its indicator: service rate, cost per kilometre-litre, batch compliance. The modular mini-refinery is a dated target, not a day-one capability: at launch the molecule is to enter through regional import into three target storage hubs — N’Djamena, Moundou, Abéché — and to be produced in-country as local refining ramps up.

Step 1

Sourcing to specification

Controlled import at start-up, local production targeted later: every batch comes in with its certificate of conformity.

Step 2

Depot & storage

Reception, custody metering, regional buffer stock: the depot absorbs the shocks of the supply corridor.

Step 3

Planned transport

Optimised rounds, a dedicated tanker fleet, telematics and road-safety rules: deliver the right volume, on time, without incident.

Step 4

Point of sale

Tchadium stations, Mobile Stations, approved resellers and business delivery: six routes to the customer, one standard.

Step 5

Quality & traceability

Batches tracked to the pump, unannounced checks, specifications on display: trust is measured, not declared.

Step 6

Service & loyalty

Shop, local services, business offers and a loyalty programme (target): the station becomes a place of life, not just a stop.

LPG · Safety

The bottle against the axe — safely.

Gas only replaces wood fuel if every cylinder inspires trust. The Tchadium deposit system comes with a controlled life cycle and simple habits at home.

Cylinder life cycle

Periodic pressure testing and requalification targeted for the deposit fleet, valve check at every pass through the centre, withdrawal of aged or corroded cylinders. A cylinder that circulates is a cylinder that is inspected.

At every refill

Cylinder weighed in front of the customer (tare and fill legible), new seal, leak check before hand-over. A Tchadium exchange point is not a swap: it is a controlled act.

At home

Compliant 28-30 mbar regulator, soapy-water test at the slightest doubt, cylinder upright and ventilated — and never a flame to look for a leak. Simple habits, repeated at every point of sale.

Target protocols · company in formation — applicable cylinder standard to be confirmed; 6 and 12.5 kg formats detailed in the shop.
