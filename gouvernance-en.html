Framework · Governance & transparency

Governance with faces uncovered, before the first barrel.

Legal form, accounts, transparency, ethics, audit and capital discipline: the framework through which EnerTchad renders account to its financial partners — development lenders, regional institutions and sector investors — from its formation.

Legal formAccounts & auditTransparencyEthicsValuesCapitalBodies
Trust is proven, not decreed. EnerTchad S.A. is a company in formation: the governance framework described here applies today to its founders and representatives. The formal bodies — board, committees, statutory auditor — are put in place at the pace of the formation; the people who compose them will be named publicly as they are formalised.
01 · Legal framework

A société anonyme under OHADA law

EnerTchad is being formed as a société anonyme (SA) under the OHADA Uniform Act — the harmonised business law of seventeen African states. This framework imposes a known, enforceable governance architecture: a general meeting of shareholders, an administrative body, an executive management and an independent statutory auditor. Legible legal ground for any international partner.

Form

SA · OHADA law

A société anonyme governed by the harmonised business law of the OHADA zone.

Anchoring

Chadian capital & talent

A national, open shareholding over its own oil rent.

Headquarters

N’Djamena, Chad

A Chadian company, operating under harmonised regional law.

02 · Accounts & audit

Auditable by design

EnerTchad’s accounts are kept under IFRS standards and intended for certification by a statutory auditor, as the OHADA SA form requires. The goal: financial statements that are legible and comparable for any lender or co-investor, from the very first raises.

Standard

IFRS accounts

Accounting to international standards, comparable from one market to another.

Control

Statutory auditor

Independent certification of the accounts, inherent to the OHADA SA form.

Traceability

Audit trail

Every target is dated; every franc raised is earmarked and verifiable.

05 · Our values

Unity, innovation, sustainability — three decision rules

Our brand promise, “Access to Energies”, is not a slogan: it is the criterion to which we submit every trade-off. Three values translate it into enforceable rules — and each can be verified in a body, a document or a figure published on this site, rather than in a statement of intent.

Unity

The chain before the silo

Upstream, midstream, downstream and petrochemicals answer to a single balance sheet. No pole may optimise its margin at the expense of the next: integration is a governance choice as much as an industrial one.

Innovation

Frugal, therefore auditable

Modular, measured, reversible. A solution is retained only if its cost, footprint and benefit can be documented — which makes it verifiable by a third party, not merely defensible internally.

Sustainability

What stays in the country

Local content, training before extraction, site restoration. The question asked of every project: what remains in Chad when the work is done?

07 · Governance bodies

Who governs EnerTchad

The OHADA SA form structures governance around four complementary bodies. Their named composition is established over the course of the formation and will be published here — names, backgrounds and responsibilities.

Sovereignty

General meeting

The shareholders: strategic direction and approval of the accounts.

Administration

Board of directors

Oversight, appointment of management, risk control.

Execution

Executive management

Operational conduct, implementation of the strategy.

Control

Statutory auditor

Independent audit and certification, provided for by OHADA law.

Governance is judged with faces uncovered. The women and men who carry EnerTchad — management and board — will be presented by name, with their backgrounds, as soon as the bodies are formalised. That is a commitment, not an omission.

08 · Committees & documents

The board committees, and the documents that ground them

Naming the bodies is not enough: what makes governance verifiable is the specialised committees that prepare decisions and the documents that set their rules. Here is the target composition of the board and the real status of each document — published, in draft, or to be established at incorporation.

Targeted

Audit committee

Prepares the closing of accounts, engages with the statutory auditor, oversees internal control and the risk map. A majority of non-executive directors is targeted.

Targeted

HSE & sustainability committee

Monitors safety and environmental performance, the indicator table and the low-carbon trajectory; convened on any major incident.

Targeted

Nomination & remuneration committee

Proposes the board's composition, safeguards the balance of skills and frames executive remuneration.

Targeted

Ethics & compliance committee

Oversees the anti-corruption framework, the whistleblowing channel and conflicts of interest, with direct access to the board.

Status of the documents

  • To be established · incorporationArticles of association of the public limited company (AUSCGIE) — published here on signature.
  • To be established · incorporationBoard rules of procedure and the charters of the four committees.
  • PublishedCode of ethics and conduct — anti-corruption, gifts, conflicts of interest, whistleblowing.
  • PublishedCharter & design system — the identity and its rules of use.
  • PublishedPayments-to-governments reporting format — seven EITI categories.
  • Forthcoming · first financial yearSustainability report and payments-to-governments report.
The named composition of the board will be published here — names, background, independence and committee membership — from the first constitutive general meeting. Until it is published, no name should be presented as binding the company: see the anti-fraud notice.
