EnerTchad · Digital-native

Chad exports its crude and imports its fuel. We invert the equation.

Discover EnerTchad ↓

EnerTchad inverts that equation: an integrated, Chadian-owned oil company under OHADA law, across the whole value chain — exploration-production, transport, modular refining, distribution and petrochemicals, powered by a digital backbone. The ambition: substitute imported fuels, capture the margin at every link, and turn a national resource into affordable energy, jobs and industry — with the value kept in Chad. Four links, four ways in.

Four words come up everywhere on this site — upstream, midstream, downstream (amont, intermédiaire, aval in French) and petrochemicals: these are the four links in the molecule’s journey, from reservoir to pump — and on to the bag of fertiliser. Looking for a service, a product or an industrial partner? Come in through the one that concerns you: Exploration & Production finds and produces the crude, Transport & storage moves it and holds it in reserve, Refining & distribution transforms it and serves it at the pump, Petrochemicals turns it into fertiliser, methanol and derivatives made in Chad. Four cross-cutting capabilities complete the picture.

Our purpose

EnerTchad is all of Chad’s energies, brought together.

Those that sleep beneath our basins and those that live in our people: the oil we produce, refine and distribute, the gas we valorise, the sun we capture, and the intelligence of those who put them to work. We gather them in a single Chadian hand, around one simple conviction — an energy is only worth the good it does.

That is why we seek harmony: between the resource and the land that bears it, between growth and the communities that host it, between industrial ambition and respect for life. From source rock to the pump, every link we integrate serves the same purpose — to put energy at the service of Chadians’ well-being and the country’s development.

A company in formation, we inherit nothing: we build, step by step, the enterprise that will turn our energies into shared progress.
