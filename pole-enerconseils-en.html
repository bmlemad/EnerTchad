Use cases · Advisory

From the real problem to the EnerTchad response

Five concrete situations from Chad’s oil sector, and how EnerTchad intends to answer them — as targets, at the pace of the stages.

  1. Case 01

    Challenge

    Poorly characterised block

    Investing in a poorly known block means betting on the subsurface.

    EnerTchad response

    Independent block study: basins, cadastre, re-interpretation of the data.

    Target

    De-risk before committing capital

  2. Case 02

    Challenge

    Public decisions without a model

    The State arbitrates without a tariff model or consolidated data.

    EnerTchad response

    Advice to operators and to the State, economic models for the sector.

    Target

    Decisions informed by data

  3. Case 03

    Challenge

    Opaque export tariff

    The cost of the export corridor remains poorly optimised.

    EnerTchad response

    Tariff modelling of the Doba–Kribi corridor and throughput scenarios.

    Target

    Optimised tariff · transparency

  4. Case 04

    Challenge

    EITI/OHADA compliance to be structured

    Operating demands a solid framework of transparency and compliance.

    EnerTchad response

    EITI support, OHADA law and reporting — compliance through the system.

    Target

    Documented compliance · funder confidence

  5. Case 05

    Challenge

    Local content hard to measure

    The real Chadian share of a project is often poorly quantified.

    EnerTchad response

    Methods for measuring local content and quantified improvement plans.

    Target

    Local content measured and managed

  6. Case 06

    Challenge

    Reserves to be certified

    An investor or a lender wants verified figures — not merely declared ones.

    EnerTchad response

    Audits & independent valuation: reserves certification, integrity, due diligence.

    Target

    Figures that stand up, sourced and dated

EnerConseils · Audits

Independent audits & evaluation

Independent audits by EnerConseils: reserves certification, asset integrity & HSE, technical due diligence, data, OPEX and compliance — SPE-PRMS anchored.

  • Independence is not a slogan.An audit is only worth its distance. At EnerConseils, the rule will be simple: the team that certifies an asset will never be the one that advised on it — whoever verifies will have nothing to sell on what they verify. Every finding will be sourced — cadastre, production histories, regulations, field measurements — and put to contradiction before it is concluded. We will write in a report only what we could verify; the rest will be flagged as a caveat, not hidden.
  • Four audit domains.Each domain answers a precise question, with enforceable deliverables and an identified audience. An audit can cover one domain or combine several.
  • An audit, in five stages.From kickoff to follow-up, an audit engagement runs a short, documented sequence. The order never changes: we never conclude before the data and the audited party have spoken.
  • Audits anchored to recognised standards.A finding is only worth as much as the language its reader speaks. Our audits rely on the frameworks investors, lenders and authorities expect — not an in-house method.
