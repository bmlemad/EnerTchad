Latest releases

N’Djamena · 15 May 2026

EnerTchad S.A. begins its formation as an integrated oil company under OHADA law

EnerTchad S.A. announces the start of its formation as a public limited company under OHADA law, with the ambition of building Chad’s oil chain — “from source rock to pump”. A single company organised in three core poles, it targets capital raised from 10 million to 1 billion, then 20 billion FCFA. All announced capacities are dated objectives; no asset is in operation at this stage.

N’Djamena · 2 June 2026

EnerTchad structures its investment partnership with GCIC

EnerTchad S.A. sets out the framework of its investment partnership with GCIC to support its first targeted capital raise. The funds would support access to mature fields, enhanced recovery from local inputs and modular refining — each floor funding the next. A company in formation: projections and amounts are objectives, not results.
