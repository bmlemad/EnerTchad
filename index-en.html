Three links, one molecule.

Three words come up everywhere on this site — upstream, midstream, downstream (amont, intermédiaire, aval in French): these are the three links in the molecule’s journey, from reservoir to pump. Petrochemicals extend the chain — from fuel to a bag of fertiliser made in Chad. Looking for a service, a product or an industrial partner? Come in through your need → — or through the link that concerns you: Exploration-Production finds and produces the crude, Transport & storage moves and stores it, and Refining & distribution transforms it and serves it at the pump. Four built-in capabilities hold the whole together.

Find your way · The chain’s supports

Four capabilities, built into the core poles.

The site is organised around three core poles — Upstream, Midstream, Downstream — petrochemicals extending Downstream. Four capabilities now live inside each of them, not beside them: GreenTech decarbonises and protects water, TchadiTech provides the digital foundation and R&D, Tchaditude trains the next Chadian generation, EnerConseils puts sector knowledge at everyone’s service. Each has its page — and its own doors in.
