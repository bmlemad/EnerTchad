Core business · Downstream · Distribution

Distribution: the link that touches every Chadian.

It is at the end of the chain — all the way to the pump — that the value of Chadian crude returns to the citizen. EnerTchad aims for a distribution model designed to give purchasing power back to Chadians by driving fuel prices among the lowest, guarantee access for the greatest number, and support the State in its energy-sovereignty policy.

Angle 01

Purchasing power & fair prices

Fuels among the cheapest, at the ARSAT price everywhere — including in the areas imports neglect. Margin control at every stage benefits the Chadian consumer first.

Angle 02

Hub-station network

Fewer stations, but better designed: hub stations with their own storage, designed to mesh the territory and secure supply all the way to remote areas.

Angle 03

Last mile & logistics

An integrated logistics cascade from local refining to the depot, then from depot to pump: each link feeds the next, down to the most isolated community.

Angle 04

Services & digital

The station becomes a place of services: e-shop, customer portal, and charging points EV charging to support tomorrow’s mobility.

How it works

From the depot to the pump, step by step.

Distribution is a chain of custody as much as a chain of logistics: every litre is metered, moved and delivered under one standard. The modular mini-refinery is a dated target, not a day-one capability: at launch the molecule enters through regional import into three storage hubs — N’Djamena, Moundou, Abéché — and is produced in-country as local refining ramps up. Here is its journey.

Step 1

Sourcing & regional import

At launch, finished products are sourced from Niger, Nigeria, Cameroon, Angola and Algeria — overland from the region, and by sea via the Douala–N’Djamena corridor.

Step 2

Depot & storage

Refined products are stored in dedicated tanks per product, with custody metering and batch traceability.

Step 3

Dispatch & logistics

Loading under metered custody, road transport and last-mile logistics — including Mobile Stations™ for enclaved areas.

Step 4

The pump

Fixed Tchadium stations and mobile units deliver at the ARSAT price, the same across the territory.

Step 5

B2B delivery

Fleets, industry and institutions are supplied on site under framework orders, with the paperwork to match. This is the launch business: mines and public works, transport fleets, agro-industry, and standby power for institutions.

Quality · Proof

A fair litre is not declared: it is proven.

The chain of proof built from field to depot — numbered seals, weighbridge checks and contradictory dipping (logistics), certificate of analysis for every batch (refining) — ends at the station. Here is the last link.

Reference sample

At every station delivery, a sealed, numbered sample is kept. If a litre is ever questioned, we compare against the depot batch — not against memories. It extends the corridor custody chain all the way to the pump.

Calibration on display

Pumps gauged and calibrated at a set frequency, with a dated metrology certificate displayed in the station. Customers do not take our word for it: they check. The fair litre is a commitment of scales, not of communication.

Marking & batch

Every batch enters with its certificate of analysis; molecular fuel marking targeted, in support of national anti-adulteration programmes, as run by East African regulators. Adulteration and the parallel market are fought in the laboratory.

Target protocols · company in formation — the custody chain and per-batch CoAs are described on the logistics and refining pages.

LPG · Safety

The bottle against the axe — safely.

Gas only replaces wood fuel if every cylinder inspires trust. The Tchadium deposit system comes with a controlled life cycle and simple habits at home.

Cylinder life cycle

Periodic pressure testing and requalification targeted for the deposit fleet, valve check at every pass through the centre, withdrawal of aged or corroded cylinders. A cylinder that circulates is a cylinder that is inspected.

At every refill

Cylinder weighed in front of the customer (tare and fill legible), new seal, leak check before hand-over. A Tchadium exchange point is not a swap: it is a controlled act.

At home

Compliant 28-30 mbar regulator, soapy-water test at the slightest doubt, cylinder upright and ventilated — and never a flame to look for a leak. Simple habits, repeated at every point of sale.

Target protocols · company in formation — NF EN 589 targeted; 6 and 12.5 kg formats detailed in the shop.
