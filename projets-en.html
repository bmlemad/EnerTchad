Our method

How we run a project: five rules

Eight flagship projects, one discipline. Whatever its size, every EnerTchad project follows the same rules of conduct — that is what lets a compact team carry an ambitious portfolio.

Rule 1

Phase, always

No project is committed as one block: short stages, each with its deliverable and its exit point — we can slow down or stop without wrecking the whole.

Rule 2

Finance in steps

Each stage cleared unlocks the next: capital is exposed progressively, at the pace of demonstrated results — never on the strength of a plan alone.

Rule 3

Build with the country

Local firms supervised, Chadian skills trained on the job: local content is not a box to tick, it is a project deliverable.

Rule 4

Measure impact, not activity

Kilometres laid, jobs created, litres delivered, hectares irrigated: every project has its outcome indicators — being busy is not moving forward.

Rule 5

Report, regularly

Progress, spend, incidents, variances: every project publishes its status at fixed intervals — partners’ trust is built by the regularity of the reporting.

How to read a status

These definitions describe individual items. Company formation, study completion and asset operation are separate milestones. They do not assign a new status to any project.

Implemented
A specific item exists, supported by a dated record.
In progress
Work has started; identify the owner and next deliverable.
Planned
An intended action, with prerequisites still to meet.
Conditional
Depends on a stated approval, agreement, financing or validation.
Not currently available
A service or resource cannot currently be accessed.
