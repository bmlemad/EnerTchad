Human capital · The builders of the next generation

Chad’s oil, developed by its own children.

Chad brims with talent — engineers, geologists, technicians, managers, trained here and across the world. EnerTchad has gathered them under one banner: for the first time, these national skills are working together to develop the Chadian oil sector, from source rock to pump. No longer as the executants of foreign operators, but as the architects of their own industry.

Train · Transform · Share
Train

Building the Chadian next generation before extracting — academy, work-study and academic partnerships.

Transform

Converting the resource into value, and the country with it — from molecule to trade, in Chad.

Share

Putting our talent and good practice at the service of Chad’s whole oil ecosystem.

Human capital · A talent-pooling hub

Shared human capital, serving the whole African oil industry.

Tchaditude does not train for EnerTchad alone: it is conceived as a pooling hub for oil skills — a shared talent pool that supports the sector’s development, covers the whole industry and weaves intra-African exchanges of experience. (vision · company in formation)

A pooled talent base
A shared pool, not a captive one

Certified talent made available to operators, service companies and institutions — skills circulate through the whole industry.

The whole chain
From source rock to pump

Geosciences, drilling, production, transport, refining, distribution, HSE-Q and data: expertise on every link of the oil chain.

Intra-African exchanges
South-South cooperation

Mobility, twinnings, cross-training and shared experience between African basins (CEMAC, Gulf of Guinea, East Africa) — learning and passing on between peers.

Supporting the sector
Development through skills

Raising the level of the whole Chadian and regional oil industry, so growth rests on competent, lasting African human capital.

“The sector’s development cannot be imported: it is built with African talent exchanging experience.”

Our local-content project

Train, before extracting.

A national industry starts with its skills. EnerTchad plans an Energy Training Centre (CFE) and academic partnerships to build, from the outset, the Chadian talent pool of the whole chain.

TRACK 01 · FIELD
E&P operations

Drilling, production, maintenance — IFP School certification targeted.

TRACK 02 · MIDSTREAM
Pipeline engineering

Integrity, SCADA, inspection — the API 1160 framework.

TRACK 03 · DOWNSTREAM
Refining process

Unit operation, quality control, process safety.

TRACK 04 · RETAIL
Distribution & network

Station management, fuel compliance, commercial fleet.

Academic partnerships targeted: IFP School, the University of N’Djamena, the IUT of Abéché, the ENS of Moundou — for work-study tracks (production, maintenance, instrumentation, HSE, finance) with senior mentors and field assignments. The goal: bringing Chadian juniors up without their having to leave the country.

Operational task force · Delegated operations

Teams ready to operate your sites.

Beyond one-off services, EnerTchad mobilises multi-disciplinary, certified, immediately operational teams to take over the running of a site — in part or in full. A local operator who knows the terrain, at a controlled cost, in place of costly expatriates.

Human capital · Training tracks

Training Chad’s next oil generation.

Tchaditude sets out to build Chadian oil’s human capital — train before extracting, through work-study and mentoring. Each card details a career track. (ambition · company in formation)

Geosciences

Reading Chad’s subsurface.

  • Geology, geophysics, interpretation.
  • Work-study between field and design office.
  • Mentoring by experienced seniors.
Use case · cost reduction

Challenge — Subsurface expertise is scarce in-country and imported at a premium.

Integrated answer — A work-study geosciences track, mentored by seniors.

Targeted gainRare skills created in Chad · fewer expatriates

Drilling & wells

The trades of the well.

  • Drilling and completion operations.
  • Well safety and procedures.
  • Progressive upskilling.
Use case · cost reduction

Challenge — The well trades depend on a costly expatriate workforce.

Integrated answer — Training in drilling/completion operations, progressive upskilling.

Targeted gainExpatriate dependence reduced · payroll localised

HSE-Q

Safety as a culture.

  • Health, safety, environment, quality.
  • The ALARP approach and stop-work authority.
  • HSE leads trained in-house.
Use case · cost reduction

Challenge — Without a safety culture, an incident costs both human and financial capital.

Integrated answer — HSE leads trained in-house, ALARP and stop-work authority spread wide.

Targeted gainFewer incidents · licence to operate preserved

Data & AI

Digital oil.

  • Field data, analysis, applied AI.
  • Supervision tools and twins.
  • Rare skills grown in-country.
Use case · cost reduction

Challenge — Digital oil skills are rare and expensive on the market.

Integrated answer — A field-applied data/AI track, tooled by TchadiTech.

Targeted gainDigital oil mastered locally

Maintenance & instrumentation

Making the facilities reliable.

  • Mechanics, electricity, instrumentation.
  • Preventive and condition-based maintenance.
  • Training workshops and benches.
Use case · cost reduction

Challenge — For lack of qualified technicians, breakdowns stop production.

Integrated answer — Maintenance/instrumentation training workshops and benches.

Targeted gainAvailability improved · external subcontracting reduced

Management & leadership

Leading tomorrow’s teams.

  • Team and project leadership.
  • HSE leadership and exemplarity.
  • A 100 % Chadian managerial next generation.
Use case · cost reduction

Challenge — The sector lacks a Chadian managerial next generation.

Integrated answer — Team leadership and HSE leadership, exemplarity in the field.

Targeted gainA 100 % Chadian next generation · company continuity
