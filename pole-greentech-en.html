The conviction · Sustainability

Decarbonising the chain without giving up the barrel.

Flaring avoided, gas-solar hybridisation of sites, produced water reinjected: every barrel produced with a smaller footprint is worth more — for the country as much as for the investor.

Decarbonising without giving up the barrel

EnerTchad’s GreenTech is not an electricity trading business: it is the tool that makes our operations cleaner and cheaper. Hybridising associated gas and solar on every site, putting produced water to use, and targeting the end of routine flaring — every green kilowatt saves a litre of diesel and a tonne of CO₂.

Our four pillars

Four pillars, a single requirement: producing while leaving less behind.

EnerTchad’s sustainability is not a separate chapter: it reads across four axes that run through every pole. Each carries its dated target and the standard it aims for — a company in formation, so objectives to be met, not results already achieved.

Where we operate

Sustainability plays out across four Chadian terrains.

A single-country operator cannot talk about the environment in general: every basin, every corridor raises its own question.

  • Doba BasinMature fields and high water cut: produced water becomes a resource there rather than a waste, and associated gas powers the sites.
  • Bongor basinMarginal blocks and neighbouring communities: local content, jobs and continuous dialogue determine the social licence to operate.
  • Lake Chad & SahelWater resources under stress and fragile biodiversity: controlled abstraction, zero discharge targeted, sites rehabilitated.
  • Doba–Kribi corridor1 070 km of evacuation: asset integrity, robotic inspection and road safety on the last kilometre.

GreenTech · HSE-Q

HSE-Q & compliance

Safety comes before production: health, safety, environment and quality (HSE-Q) and the compliance framework EnerTchad is targeting.

  • Safety comes before production.Before the first barrel, EnerTchad builds its HSE-Q culture: a stop-work authority granted to everyone, an ALARP logic (risk as low as reasonably practicable) and targeted alignment with the major international frameworks. (target bedrock · certification targe
  • Safety, the licence to operate.In oil, HSE is not an annex: it is the condition of the barrel. EnerTchad models its target arrangements on the industry’s international standards (IOGP), adapted to Chadian terrain — six disciplines that will apply on every site, from well to pump.
  • Named life-saving rules, the same for everyone.The STOP right protects when danger is already there. Life-saving rules exist so it never gets that far. Inspired by the IOGP reference set — the industry pooled them because contractors do most of the field work — they are few, displayed, and non-negotiable.
  • Learning before the accident, not after.A near-miss is an accident that gave a warning. A learning organisation treats it as free ore — provided that reporting it costs nothing to the person who saw it.

  • Two Chadian wonders.Eighteen interconnected lakes in the hyper-arid Sahara, fed by a fossil aquifer — a hydrological phenomenon unique in the world, with blue, green and red waters, in the heart of Ennedi-Borkou.
