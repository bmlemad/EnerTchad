Distribution network · The cash engine · Product

Fewer stations, but better designed.

Our strategy is not to line up as many stations as possible, but to guarantee energy access to the greatest number of Chadians. Ultra-modern hub stations, each on one hectare with its own strategic storage — together they form a distributed strategic reserve for the country — ensuring fair prices and stable supply; mobile stations extend that network to landlocked areas. It is this model, light on capital and strong on margin, that generates the company’s cash flow.

“Proximity is our distribution doctrine: if the customer cannot come to the energy, the energy goes to the customer — at the same price, everywhere.”

Our distribution model · At a glance

A single flow, from the local refinery to the last mile.

Rather than a scatter of stations, EnerTchad designs its network as an integrated logistics cascade: each link feeds the next, from the strategic depot to the most isolated community. Fewer tied-up assets, more resilience, a controlled margin at every stage — and security of supply designed against disruptions.

Local supply & depot ↑ Click a tier to explore

Supply prioritises local refining — our modular mini-refineries — with regulated imports serving only as a transition complement. Fuel is stored in one-hectare hub-depots (N’Djamena, Moundou, Abéché): buy at the right time, smooth out shortages and hold a stable price.

Deployment plan · National mesh

A network in three tiers, structured by demographics and the corridors.

Siting follows three national realities: the concentration of population in the south (nearly half of Chadians live in the southernmost fifth of the territory), the single Douala–N’Djamena import corridor coming up through Moundou, and the logistics break-bulk nodes. Our ultra-premium stations will not be mere points of sale: they will be hub-depots supplying their area’s satellite stations in bulk.

Commercial approach · International standards

Sell hydrocarbons as a brand, not as a commodity.

The world’s downstream leaders do not sell “fuel”: they sell a branded additivised fuel, a station experience, a loyalty programme and fleet-management solutions. EnerTchad transposes these four levers to the Chadian market — to build private-customer loyalty, capture high-margin B2B and hold fair prices.

EnerTchad+ additivised fuel

Becoming a Tchadium dealer: a trade, not just a sign.

HSE · Station

The safe station, in concrete terms.

“Fire safety and tank management” is not a slogan. Here is what the safety of a Tchadium station covers, post by post.

On the forecourt

Engine off, no flame or phone at the nozzle, delivery zones cordoned during unloading, checked, visible extinguishers. The rule protects the customer as much as the attendant.

Under the slab

Double-wall tanks with leak detection targeted, compliant vents and vapour recovery under study, tight stock dipping: a level gap is an alert, not a surprise.

If things go wrong

Emergency stop cutting the pumps, spill kit on site, staff drilled on fire and spill scenarios, instructions displayed. We practise before, not during.
