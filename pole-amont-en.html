Our approach

Frugal, integrated, sovereign: the Exploration & Production method

Five principles guide every decision of the hub — from the first gigabyte of seismic to the last barrel recovered. A method designed for a landlocked country, mature reservoirs and capital that is counted.

01

De-risking through data

Before any heavy commitment: re-interpretation of public data, targeted seismic where uncertainty runs high, reservoir model. The decision to invest is taken on measured facts, never on a geological bet.

02

Targeting the low-capital barrel

Mature fields, marginal blocks, wells to re-enter: priority goes to the barrels reachable fast and at lower CAPEX, at the pace of capital raises — no megaproject, self-financed stages.

03

Integrating under a single contract

From earthworks to the pump, eight OFS services and ten well intervention lines under a single point of contact: fewer interfaces, faster mobilisation, a barrel cost controlled end to end.

04

Producing with the country

Local inputs — natron, lime, gum arabic, neem —, Chadian teams trained through Tchaditude, national subcontracting: every barrel produced strengthens the economy around it.

05

Measure, learn, redeploy

Every pilot is instrumented: recovery factor, OPEX per barrel, HSE indicators. What works is replicated from well to well; what disappoints is stopped early, before it drains capital.

Use cases · Exploration & Production

From the real problem to the EnerTchad response

Five concrete situations from Chad’s oil sector, and how EnerTchad intends to answer them — as targets, at the pace of the stages.

  1. Case 01

    Challenge

    Mature field in decline

    A reservoir in the Doba/Bongor basins is losing pressure and flow rate.

    EnerTchad response

    Enhanced oil recovery (EOR) with local inputs — natron-based ASP, digital reservoir monitoring.

    Target

    +8 to 17% recovery factor targeted

  2. Case 02

    Challenge

    Neglected marginal block

    A block judged too small by the majors sits undeveloped.

    EnerTchad response

    Frugal take-over + integrated oilfield services (OFS) force under a single contract.

    Target

    Fast production on low initial capital

  3. Case 03

    Challenge

    Geological uncertainty

    The subsurface is poorly characterised: investing means betting.

    EnerTchad response

    Targeted seismic, re-interpretation of public data and de-risking models.

    Target

    Deciding before committing capital

  4. Case 04

    Challenge

    Scattered production data

    Well histories are dispersed — paper, silos, mismatched formats.

    EnerTchad response

    Digitisation and a unified reservoir model, real-time production dashboard.

    Target

    Running the field on reliable data

  5. Case 05

    Challenge

    Poorly anticipated well abandonment

    Closing an end-of-life well is a cost that is often overlooked.

    EnerTchad response

    Abandonment plans from the design stage and reuse of existing infrastructure.

    Target

    Life cycle under control · abandonment cost provisioned
