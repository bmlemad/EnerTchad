01 · Identity

Who we are

EnerTchad S.A. is an integrated oil company, with Chadian capital and talent, in formation as a public limited company under OHADA law. Its ambition: covering the whole value chain — from exploration-production to refining, from transport to distribution — to put the oil industry at the service of Chad’s development and retain in the country a value exported for too long. It was founded by Chadian oil and gas professionals, trained within the big international companies (E&P majors and service companies) and returned to build at home, answering the call of the highest authorities inviting Chad’s children to invest their know-how in national development. EnerTchad intends to combine the majors’ standards — safety, rigour, transparency — with an intimate knowledge of the Chadian field.

To date, EnerTchad is a company in formation: it holds no operating assets. The projects, capacities and indicators presented are dated ambitions and targets, never achieved results.

02 · Mission

Our mission

Producing, transforming and distributing reliable energy accessible to all Chadians. Chad exports most of its crude and imports its fuels: we invert that logic — serving the national market first, exporting only the surplus, and as finished products rather than crude.

Giving back purchasing power to Chadians — aiming for fuel at the fairest, most accessible price, everywhere, at the ARSAT price.

Valorising in Chad
Refining and transforming crude and gas on national soil rather than exporting them raw.
Opening up
Aiming for fuel access in the 23 provinces, out to rural areas.
Leveraging
Turning the oil rent into jobs, training and infrastructure.

03 · Vision

Our vision

Our natural resources must serve as a lever of growth and development.

Making Chad an integrated, national and sustainable oil player in Central Africa. Aiming, beyond 2030, to master the whole value chain on national soil — from source rock to the pump — so that oil wealth funds the country’s development rather than leaving it raw.

“Our vision is not to become billionaires — but to help build Chad’s energy autonomy, by giving everyone access to energy.” Capital is not an end: it is the means of reaching our objectives for the country.

03a · The approach

“Chad’s energies, in our hands.”

This is not one more slogan: it is our method. “In our hands” means in Chadian hands — and that can be verified on four planes, like four fingers of the same hand.

Our capital. A company under Chadian law, with Chadian capital: the margin of every link taken over — refining, transport, distribution — stays in the country instead of leaving with the crude. Investors →

Our skills. 80% Chadian staff targeted, and an entire hub — Tchaditude — to train, certify and federate the next generation: know-how stops being imported by the hour.

Our decisions. The trade-offs are made in N’Djamena, at the country’s pace and to its benefit — in partnership with the State, SHT and the operators, never in their place. Governance →

Our data. Collected in the field, hosted and used in-country by TchadiTech: digital sovereignty is the extension of energy sovereignty.

Taking things in hand is not closing the door: it is the condition of sharing. It is because the chain is mastered here that “Access to Energy” can be promised to all — from the main road to the isolated village.

Our choices

What we decided not to do.

03a-bis · The single-country choice

Single-country, by design.

EnerTchad is designed for Chad, by Chadians. A single-country scope is not a constraint we put up with: it is what makes full commitment possible.

One field, all the engineering. A company serving one country devotes all of its engineering to a single context: the geology of the Doba and Bongor basins, heavy crudes and their discount, the export corridor to Kribi, the distances, the climate, the skills available on the ground. Where a multi-country operator adapts recipes designed elsewhere, EnerTchad aims for solutions drawn for this ground.

Getting more from the country’s resources. That focus has a measurable objective: drawing more from what already exists — enhanced recovery on mature fields, valuing gas flared today, produced water treated and reinjected, refining capacity sized for local demand — rather than chasing new discoveries.

Access to the best technologies. A single country does not mean second-rate technology, quite the opposite: EnerTchad aims for access to the best available oil production technologies, chosen because they suit Chad — its geology, its volumes, its inputs, its skills — and not because they suit everywhere. The selection criterion is fit to context, not the supplier’s reputation.

EnerTchad S.A. is a company in formation: the capabilities described here are objectives and design choices, not activities under way.

05 · Genesis & history

Our genesis, step by step

EnerTchad is born of an observation and a return. The observation: too many Chadians endure expensive fuel and territories without reliable energy. The return: that of Chadian engineers and managers, back home to build, from source rock to the pump, their nation’s oil chain. Here are the milestones of this beginning story.
