EnerTchad — institutional overview

Essentials · 90 seconds

Understand EnerTchad at a glance.

EnerTchad is an integrated oil company under OHADA law, currently in formation in Chad. Its ambition is to connect production, transport, transformation, distribution and cross-functional capabilities so more energy value is retained in the country. Volumes, capacities and timelines presented as objectives remain targets until achieved.
