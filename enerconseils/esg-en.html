Positioning · Why us

The ESG we sell is the one we apply to ourselves.

EnerTchad is building an integrated oil chain with dated ESG commitments: 30 %+ on-site renewables targeted, zero routine flaring targeted, an academy training Chad’s next generation, OHADA governance with EITI alignment targeted. That internal discipline has become a capability — three poles carry it, and put it at the service of other companies.

GreenTech · environmentOn-site solar, water management, environmental monitoring, decarbonisation: the sustainability pole designs and deploys the field solutions.
EnerConseils · governanceDiagnostics, compliance, extra-financial reporting and independent audits — backed by the sector Atlas and its sourced data.
Tchaditude · socialLocal content, training, community dialogue: the academy and its partnerships turn social obligations into lasting skills.
Offer · Six services

Six ESG services, from diagnostic to field.

Each service answers a precise question, with dated deliverables and a named owner. An engagement can cover one service or combine them into a programme.

Service 01

ESG diagnostic & roadmap

Environment-social-governance baseline, materiality matrix, gaps against the frameworks you target (ISO, GRI, IFC), prioritisation. You leave with a dated, costed action plan — not a report that sleeps.

Who it is for

Any company structuring its ESG approach: industry, mining, banks, telecoms, institutions.

Deliverables

  • ESG diagnostic report
  • Materiality matrix
  • 12-24 month costed roadmap
Service 02

Carbon accounting & decarbonisation

GHG accounting (relevant scopes 1, 2 and 3), a prioritised reduction plan, on-site solar hybridisation, hunting flaring and leaks for operators. Every tonne avoided is measured, not proclaimed.

Who it is for

Oil and oilfield-services operators, energy-intensive industry, fleets and logistics.

Deliverables

  • Documented GHG inventory
  • Costed decarbonisation plan
  • Climate-finance dossier
Service 03

HSE-Q compliance & systems

Building or upgrading the health-safety-environment-quality management system: procedures, risk mapping, emergency plans, safety culture, preparation for ISO 14001 / 45001 certification.

Who it is for

Industrial sites, worksites, depots and carriers under contractual HSE requirements.

Deliverables

  • HSE-Q manual & procedures
  • Risk mapping
  • Internal audit programme
Service 04

Local content & social impact

Local-content plan, local purchasing and subcontracting, community dialogue, training plans backed by the Tchaditude academy. The social obligation becomes an anchor — and an argument.

Who it is for

Operators under local-content obligations, infrastructure projects, lenders.

Deliverables

  • Local-content plan
  • Social impact study
  • Training & mentoring programme
Service 05

Governance & extra-financial reporting

Ethics charter and anti-corruption arrangements, OHADA compliance, EITI alignment, first ESG report and indicator dashboard. Your extra-financial performance, in your financiers’ language.

Who it is for

Executive teams, boards, companies raising funds or bidding for contracts.

Deliverables

  • Governance charter & procedures
  • Annual ESG report
  • Indicator dashboard
Service 06

GreenTech field solutions

Solarising sites and buildings, water management, environmental and biodiversity monitoring backed by the Atlas, site remediation. The part of ESG you can see — and that cuts the energy bill.

Who it is for

Remote or energy-hungry sites, mining, agro-industry, telecom operators, local authorities.

Deliverables

  • Solarisation study & sizing
  • Environmental management plan
  • Monitoring protocol & indicators
Method · How it runs

An ESG engagement, in five steps.

From first exchange to annual report, an engagement follows a short, documented sequence. The order never changes: we never recommend before we have measured.

Step 1

Listening & scope

Your obligations, your financiers, your deadlines: the question is framed in writing, with scope, calendar and expected deliverables.

Step 2

Field diagnostic & data

Site visits, measurements, interviews, document review: every finding will have its source. Sector data leans on the Atlas.

Step 3

Costed roadmap

Prioritised actions, costs, owners, deadlines and indicators — validated with your management, not left on a corner of a desk.

Step 4

Supported implementation

Team training, field-solution deployment, certification preparation: we stay committed to the result, at your side.

Step 5

Measurement & annual report

Indicators are read, the ESG report is published, the roadmap is adjusted. ESG becomes a rhythm, not a one-shot.

Framework · Standards

Your commitments, in your financiers’ language.

An ESG approach only has value if those who finance you, rate you or audit you can read it. We work on the expected frameworks — not a home-made method.

ISO 14001 / 45001Environmental and health-and-safety management systems: the base of the certifications your principals expect.
ISO 26000 & GRISocial responsibility and sustainability reporting: the grammar of an ESG report readable internationally.
IFC Performance StandardsThe eight environmental and social standards any lender-financed project will have to demonstrate.
EITI & OHADAExtractive-industries transparency and business law: the governance framework of the region.
GHG inventoryCarbon accounting by scopes: the base of any credible reduction target and any climate finance.
SDGsThe Sustainable Development Goals as an impact compass — SDG 7 above all, clean and affordable energy.

Questions · ESG

What we are asked most often.

The five questions that come up before an ESG engagement — short answers, no detours.

Are you already operational?EnerTchad is a company in formation: this offer describes the services we are building, carried by the GreenTech, EnerConseils and Tchaditude poles. Pre-figuration engagements can be scoped today, with dated commitments in writing.
Why entrust your ESG to an oil company?Because we apply these requirements to our own chain — on-site solar, zero routine flaring targeted, a training academy, OHADA governance and EITI alignment targeted. You are talking to practitioners, not theorists.
Do you work outside the oil sector?Yes. The offer addresses oil and oilfield-services operators, but also mining, industry, banks, telecoms and institutions — ESG frameworks are largely shared.
How long does an ESG engagement take?A diagnostic runs four to eight weeks; a roadmap deploys over twelve to twenty-four months with measurement points. The calendar is set in writing at the start.
How do you separate ESG advisory from independent audit?As for our audits: the team that certifies has nothing to sell on what it certifies. An ESG advisory engagement and an audit on the same scope are carried by distinct teams, and the separation is set in writing.
