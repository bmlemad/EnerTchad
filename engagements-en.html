Framework · Before the first barrel

Safety, quality and compliance before the first barrel.

At EnerTchad, safety, quality and compliance are not options: they are the foundations of bankability. The organisation, controls and recognised standards (OHADA, SPE-PRMS, ISO, API) are adopted from formation — the framework funders and co-investors demand before the first barrel, so that growth is never improvised. Here are the four commitments that run through all our trades.

TargetISO, API and EITI alignment where stated
OperationsNo operating asset declared to date
ReportingDated ESG and 2030 targets are intended to make progress auditable

Our climate ambition. Operations low-carbon by design: routine flaring avoided, site solar and minimal carbon intensity targeted — so that valorising Chadian oil serves development without mortgaging the future. Dated targets of a company in formation, not results.

01 · Health · Safety · Environment

HSE

An ALARP approach (“as low as reasonably practicable”) applicable to every operation, and environmental protection anchored in Chad’s realities — the water of Lake Chad and the Sahel, the soils, the air.

The stop-work right
Anyone can stop an operation deemed dangerous: production never comes before people.
An end to routine flaring targeted
Valorising associated gas rather than burning it; cutting emissions at the source.
Water & zero spills targeted
Treatment and valorisation of produced water, reinjection, a zero-discharge objective.
ALARP approachISO 14001 certification targetedISO 45001 certification targetedGRI · IPIECA targeted

Details: indicators and rules of the HSE framework

Health

Protecting every worker’s health.

  • Occupational medicine and exposure monitoring (targeted).
  • Industrial hygiene: noise, dust, the heat of the Sahel.
  • Fatigue prevention and rotation management.

Safety

Production never comes before people.

  • Process safety: barriers and equipment integrity.
  • Work permits and lockout (LOTO).
  • Risk analysis before every task (JSA) + stop-work right.

Environment

A minimal footprint, anchored in Chad.

  • Water: treatment, reinjection — a zero-spill objective.
  • Air: an end to routine flaring targeted, emissions controlled.
  • Soils & biodiversity: a reduced footprint, site rehabilitation.

03 · Operational safety

Operational safety

Safety is not an added option: it is built into the design of operations, from isolated sites to the last kilometre of distribution.

People’s safety
A stop-work right for everyone, continuous HSE training backed by Tchaditude, a reporting culture.
Road & logistics safety
Journey management, a specialised tanker fleet, hydrocarbon-transport compliance.
Facility integrity
Inspection of the facilities EnerTchad operates (depots, gathering lines): pigs, drones, SCADA supervision, “zero trust” OT cybersecurity.

Double materiality

An issue is a priority if it weighs both ways: on the company’s value, and on the world around it. That is the reading of recent standards (CSRD / ESRS), and the one EnerTchad uses to arbitrate its E-S-G priorities.

Financial materiality
What weighs on value: water access in the Sahel, continuity of the Doba ↔ Kribi corridor, regulated ARSAT prices, site and people safety, the social licence to operate.

The grievance mechanism

A grievance that gets lost is a risk that grows. The target mechanism is simple, dated and traceable — and substitutes for neither the courts nor the whistleblower channel.

1 · Receive
A channel accessible without mandatory writing: a visit, a phone call, a community relay. Every grievance is recorded.
2 · Acknowledge
A dated acknowledgement, with a named contact — the person knows their word has entered the system.
3 · Investigate
Fact analysis, a site visit if needed, a reasoned decision — within a timeframe announced at acknowledgement.
4 · Answer & trace
An explained answer, corrective measures tracked, a consolidated register reviewed by management — without reprisals, ever.
Aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals (UN): SDG 7 — affordable, clean energy · SDG 8 — decent work and growth · SDG 13 — climate action · SDG 16 — effective, transparent institutions.

Governance · Proof

Figures you can verify without us.

The 2030 targets publish, for every indicator, its baseline, its target and how it is measured. The missing storey was independence. The accounts will be certified by a statutory auditor; the non-financial data will have its own verification.

External assurance targeted

ISAE 3000-type limited assurance is targeted on the key indicators of the annual sustainability report: an independent engagement, a published scope, conclusions attached to the report. We do not grade ourselves.

Revenues under EITI

Payments to governments — royalties, taxes, dividends — declared by category and reconciled with public registers, within the targeted EITI support. The rent is verified on both sides of the counter.

Auditable at the source

Every indicator traces back to a time-stamped primary record: flaring telemetry, energy metering, HR and procurement registers. The logic of certificates of analysis and custody transfer, applied to non-financial data: no figure without an audit trail.
