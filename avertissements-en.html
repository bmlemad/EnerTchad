Company in formation · forward-looking information

EnerTchad S.A. is an integrated oil company in the process of formation. To date, it operates no assets. The contents of this site describe a project and ambitions, not an operating activity or achieved results.

Forward-looking statements

This site contains forward-looking statements: objectives, quantified targets, timetables, capital roadmaps and projections (volumes, capacities, milestones, raise amounts). These elements rest on assumptions as at the publication date and are, by nature, subject to hazards and uncertainties — technical, regulatory, financial, market and geopolitical. Actual achievements may differ materially from the ambitions presented. None of these statements constitutes a guarantee of results.

No public offering

Information on the capital, the investment memorandum or subscription opportunities is provided for general information. It constitutes neither a public offering of securities, nor a solicitation to invest, nor investment advice. Any eventual process would take place within a separate contractual framework, compliant with applicable law (OHADA and Chadian regulation). Any potential investor is invited to conduct their own due diligence and consult their advisers.

Risk factors

Investing in a company in formation carries significant risks, up to and including the total loss of the amount invested. Before any decision, each potential investor must review, at a minimum, the following families of risks:

  • Company in formation & execution. EnerTchad operates no assets yet: the poles, figures and milestones presented are targets. They may not materialise, or may materialise later, differently or for different amounts.
  • Financing. The trajectory (≈ 1 Bn then 20 Bn FCFA) rests on successive fund-raisings, none of which is guaranteed; their failure or delay would compromise the business plan.
  • Operational & technical. Exploration-production, enhanced oil recovery (EOR) with local inputs, modular refining: complex operations whose pilots may not confirm the expected performance.
  • Market & prices. Volatile crude prices, regulated downstream prices (ARSAT), the logistics costs of a landlocked country: actual margins may differ materially from the assumptions.
  • Regulatory & political. Permits, block awards, taxation, local content and regional stability rest on public decisions that may change.
  • Liquidity. The securities of an unlisted company benefit from no organised market: the investment must be considered long-term, with no certainty of resale.
  • Currency. Part of the sector’s costs and price references is denominated in dollars, while the FCFA is pegged to the euro: currency movements may affect results.

This list is not exhaustive. It complements the anti-fraud notice and the “No public offering” section above. Each investor is invited to conduct their own due diligence and consult their legal and financial advisers before any decision.

Anti-fraud notice

EnerTchad S.A. is a company in formation: to date, no payment channel is open — neither to subscribe to the capital, nor to reserve products, nor for recruitment “processing fees”.

EnerTchad never asks for cash, Mobile Money transfers, cryptocurrency or payment to an intermediary or “agent”. Any solicitation of that kind — call, WhatsApp, social media, e-mail — is an attempted fraud by impersonation of our identity.

Our only official channels: this site (enertchad-delta.vercel.app), the address contact@enertchad.td and the official WhatsApp +235 99 29 86 96. Future subscription arrangements will be announced exclusively here, with the required regulatory approvals.

In case of doubt or a suspicious solicitation, report it to us before any payment — and make no payment.
