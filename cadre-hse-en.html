Sustainability · Health, safety, environment

HSE framework

EnerTchad S.A. is in formation: no licences, no assets, no operations, no employees on site. There are therefore no hours worked and no HSE indicators to report to date. This page sets out what we will measure, the rules set before the first worksite and the table we will publish.

What we will measure

The indicators below follow the definitions of IOGP, the International Association of Oil & Gas Producers, which publishes the safety rates of its members and their contractors every year. They will cover our employees and our contractors’ employees, as IOGP reporting requires.

IndicatorDefinition (IOGP / API)UnitPublication
Hours workedActual hours worked onshore, including overtime; employees and contractors. The denominator of every rate.Hours, rounded to the nearest thousandFrom the first worksite
TRIR (total recordable injury rate)Fatalities + lost work day cases + restricted work day cases + medical treatment cases, per hours workedPer million hours workedFrom the first worksite
LTIF (lost time injury frequency)Fatalities + lost work day cases, per hours workedPer million hours workedFrom the first worksite

Tier 1 process safety eventsLoss of primary containment from a process with the most serious consequences (lost time injury or fatality, third-party hospital admission, declared evacuation, fire or explosion above a damage threshold) or a release above the Tier 1 threshold quantity in one hour (IOGP Report 456, API RP 754)Number, and rate per million drilling and production hoursFrom the first worksite
Tier 2 process safety eventsLoss of primary containment with lesser consequences: recordable injury, fire or explosion above a lower threshold, release above the Tier 2 threshold quantityNumber, and rate per million drilling and production hoursFrom the first worksite

Source of the definitions: IOGP, 2018su reporting user guide (latest freely available edition; the 2024su and 2025su editions are distributed via a form), Report 456 and Report 2024p for process safety. According to IOGP, its thresholds and consequences are consistent with API RP 754, whose 3rd edition reclassified materials and clarified definitions: we will apply the edition in force at the first worksite.

Two calculation conventions. IOGP reports TRIR and LTIF per million hours, and FAR per 100 million hours. The US OSHA reports its rates per 200,000 hours, i.e. 100 full-time workers: for the same data, an OSHA rate is five times lower than an IOGP rate. We will publish on the IOGP basis and state it next to every figure.

Flaring and methane indicators (flared volumes, methane intensity, OGMP 2.0 level) have their own page: methane & flaring.

IOGP publishes its members’ safety indicators every year in its “Safety performance indicators” reports (Report 2025s).

The rules before the first worksite

These are the rules the site already publishes in our HSE commitments and in the HSE-Q foundation of our Exploration & Production services. They will apply to every worker, employee or contractor, from the first day of the first worksite.

Stop-work authority

Anyone can stop an operation deemed dangerous: production never comes before people.

Permit to work

Systematic work permits: written authorisation before any hazardous intervention.

Lockout (LOTO)

Isolate energy sources and verify zero energy before work begins.

Risk analysis

A risk analysis before every task (JSA), shared with the crew that carries it out.

IOGP Life-Saving Rules, targeted

IOGP’s Life-Saving Rules (Report 459) are nine simple rules, each tied to an activity that has caused fatal incidents in the industry. According to IOGP, 85% of its members’ fatal incidents in 2025 related to breaches of one or more of these rules (IOGP, 1 July 2026). EnerTchad aims to adopt them unchanged, for its employees and its contractors alike:

  • Bypassing Safety Controls
  • Confined Space
  • Driving
  • Energy Isolation
  • Hot Work
  • Line of Fire
  • Safe Mechanical Lifting
  • Work Authorisation
  • Working at Height

Source: IOGP, Life-Saving Rules poster.

Management systems targeted

ISO 45001

The international standard that specifies requirements for an occupational health and safety management system (ISO 45001:2018). Certification targeted, not obtained.

ISO 14001

The international standard for environmental management systems, whose 4th edition (ISO 14001:2026) was published in April 2026. Certification targeted, not obtained.

Governance

The board plans an HSE & sustainability committee (targeted): it will monitor safety and environmental performance and the indicator table, and will be convened on any major incident.

The Chadian framework

Law No. 006/PR/2007 on hydrocarbons (consolidated text, in French) requires the contractor to carry out its works with due regard to environmental protection and to “la sécurité du personnel et des installations” (the safety of personnel and facilities, art. 52.1) and, absent specific regulations, to act according to sound practices recognised in the petroleum industry (art. 52.3). It requires an environmental impact study (art. 59), insurance against environmental accident risks (art. 61.1), and an annual report to the minister covering the environmental condition of sites and workers’ conditions (art. 54.3). We found no obligation to publish safety rates: publishing them is a voluntary commitment by EnerTchad.
