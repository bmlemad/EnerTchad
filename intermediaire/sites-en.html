Modular refining · Innovation

Modular & movable refineries, right next to the resource.

Not every field justifies a pipeline tie-in. Our modular, skid-mounted, prefabricated and transportable refining units process on site the crude of satellite basins, isolated blocks and marginal fields — converting otherwise uneconomic resources into petroleum products marketable locally. Refining strictly belongs to the Refining & Distribution pole: these mobile units appear in the Transport & Storage chapter because they deploy like infrastructure of the interior, right next to the unconnected fields.

Prefabricated & transportable

Containerised units built in the factory, delivered by road and assembled on site in a few weeks.

Scalable capacity

From a few hundred to several thousand b/d: modules are added as the field develops.

Redeployable

Once a field is depleted, the unit is dismantled and redeployed on a new site — a mobile asset, not a stranded one.

Value produced on the spot

Diesel, kerosene and fuel oil refined locally: less transport, supply for the landlocked regions.

Mini-refinery 2.0 · Frugal design

Cheaper to build, simpler to maintain.

The Mobile Station™ philosophy applied to refining: standardise, fractionalise, swap — rather than building industrial cathedrals.

Cost · fractional capex
Start small, grow by trains
  • ▸Standardised trains of 500–2,000 b/d: one design, replicated — each copy costs less than the last
  • ▸Factory prefabrication, zero heavy civil works — skids set on a slab, hooked up in weeks
  • ▸Catalogue parts (off-the-shelf pumps, valves, exchangers) — no exotic component
  • ▸Metallurgy exception: Doba crude is acidic (high acid number) — wetted parts are in steels resistant to naphthenic corrosion. Frugality applies to the architecture, not to the critical materials
  • ▸Self-consumed energy (site gas) — reduced opex, zero logistics diesel
Maintenance · standard exchange
Swap the module, don’t stop the plant
  • ▸Swap < 24 h: the failed module is exchanged for a spare, then repaired at the central N’Djamena workshop — as in aviation
  • ▸A common parts stock for the whole fleet: one store, one reference per component
  • ▸IoT sensors + SCADA: predictive maintenance — intervene before the breakdown, not after
  • ▸Chadian, Tchaditude-trained operators and maintainers — site autonomy, no captive expatriate
24 h/24
Designed to run continuously — supervised by AI

Each train carries its own sensors (temperature, pressure, flow, vibration) and a layer of artificial intelligence that optimises costs in real time — tuning yields to the incoming crude and the demand — and watches over operational integrity: drift caught before the breakdown, maintenance predictive rather than endured, shutdowns scheduled rather than improvised.

Multimodal logistics · Isolated basins

When the pipeline doesn’t pay, the road takes over.

Not every field justifies a pipeline tie-in. For isolated or low-volume basins and blocks, EnerTchad plans to evacuate crude with a fleet of purpose-built, latest-generation tanker trucks — flexible logistics that make otherwise stranded fields workable.

TANKERS
MC 306 / DOT 407 standards

Aluminium or stainless tankers designed for crude and refined products, compliant with international hydrocarbon-transport standards.

SAFETY
Geolocation & ADR

Real-time GPS tracking, overfill valves, safety valves and drivers trained in dangerous-goods transport.

FLEXIBILITY
Evacuation on demand

Delivery to the Djermaya refinery, the depots or the pipeline tie-in points, without heavy infrastructure investment.

SOBRIETY
Recent engines, low emissions

A latest-generation fleet with reduced consumption and emissions, optimised by route planning to limit empty runs.

Road transport · HSE & integrity

Specialised tanker trucks, safe by design.

Where the pipeline doesn’t go, the last kilometre travels by road: a target fleet of tanker trucks designed first for the safety and integrity of hydrocarbon transport. (target fleet · company in formation)

Our method

How a site enters service: five milestones

A depot or terminal is not improvised: from picking the land to the first receipt, every site clears the same milestones — and only moves to the next once the last one is solid.

Milestone 1

Pick the land

Local demand, road access, flood risk, distance to dwellings: the best site is a documented trade-off, not a land opportunity.

Milestone 2

Design & permit

Hazard studies, bunding and distance standards, permits and consultations: compliance is built on the drawing board, not in catch-up.

Milestone 3

Build under control

Local firms supervised, quality checks at every critical step — welds, sealing, electrical earthing: what gets buried must be beyond reproach before it is.

Milestone 4

Commission

Tank pressure tests, meter calibration, operator training, an initial emergency drill: the site receives its first litre only after its full demonstration.

Milestone 5

Operate & review

Rounds, telemetry, periodic safety reviews and material balances: a site in service remains a site under watch — and every incident avoided elsewhere enriches its standards.
