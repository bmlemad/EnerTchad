Petrochemicals · Complex & units

Complex & units

Six chained units, three investment phases: the industrial trajectory that takes Sédigui gas and the crude’s fractions from the flare to the bag of fertiliser. (2026-2030+ targets)

Discuss this project →See the detail ↓
Complex & units · EnerTchad
The sequence

Six units, one molecule

Each unit takes the previous one’s output. That is what makes the complex coherent — and what forces it to be built in order, from the most mature to the most capital-intensive.

Unit 01

Gas treatment & separation

Dehydration, NGL recovery, LPG extraction. The clean feed that supplies everything else — and the moment Sédigui gas stops being flared.

Phase 1 · the foundation
Unit 02

Ammonia

Steam reforming of methane yields syngas, then ammonia: the mother molecule of every nitrogen fertiliser.

Phase 1 · the hinge
Unit 03

Urea & NPK

Urea granulation and NPK blending. The bag of fertiliser made in-country, close to the field it is meant to feed.

Phase 2 · agricultural market
Unit 04

Methanol

The same syngas yields methanol: a solvent, a chemical feedstock, a backup fuel — and a product that exports.

Phase 2 · added value
Unit 05

Sulphur & bitumen

Sulphur recovered from gas treatment, heavy refining residues upgraded into road bitumen. Nothing goes to waste.

Phase 2 · co-products
Unit 06

Polymers & surfactants

The most capital-intensive unit: polymers and surfactants for EOR and industry, from the available cuts.

Phase 3 · depth
The sequencing

Three phases, from most mature to most capital-intensive

A petrochemical industry cannot be decreed in one block. Each phase funds the next, and each has its gate condition — without it, you do not move on to the next step.

Phasing of the petrochemical complex

The conditions

What each unit presupposes

A petrochemical complex does not stand on the molecule alone. Four conditions govern the move from plan to concrete.

Condition 01

The molecule

Contracted access to Sédigui gas and to the crude’s fractions, over a period compatible with the units’ amortisation.

Upstream · supply agreement
Condition 02

Power and water

A stable power supply and a closed water cycle — petrochemicals consume water, and the Sahel has none to spare.

GreenTech · closed cycle
Condition 03

Land and logistics

A site arbitrated between proximity to the resource and access to the Doba ↔ Kribi corridor, with the easements and heavy access that come with it.

Midstream · corridor
Condition 04

Skills and licences

Licensed processes and, above all, operators trained in-country — the Academy trains before the units run.

Tchaditude · train first
