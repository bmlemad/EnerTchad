  • Depot network — the distributed reserve: 3 targeted depot hubs, backed by the supply routes.
  • Fleet & supervision — HSE-compliant trucks, SCADA telemetry for remote sites.
  • Corridor know-how — detailed knowledge of the 1,842 km Douala–N’Djamena route (World Bank) and of the Doba–Kribi line: border posts, seasons, hard points.
