01 · Our purpose

Fighting energy poverty in Chad.

Too many Chadians face expensive fuel, supply disruptions and territories without reliable energy. EnerTchad intends to push back that insecurity by producing, refining and distributing energy at home, at the regulated ARSAT price, down to the last kilometre.

02 · The integrated chain

One company, the whole chain.

UpstreamExploration & production · enhanced oil recovery (EOR) with local inputs · oilfield services
MidstreamTransport · Doba–Kribi corridor (≈1,070 km) · strategic storage
DownstreamModular mini-refineries 500–2,000 b/d · Tchadium network · ARSAT distribution

Every link completed in Chad keeps a margin in the country. A barrel produced, transported, refined and distributed at home is worth more than a barrel exported crude: the economics of integration, applied to the national resource.

03 · Eight poles, one signature each

How the group is organised.

Upstream

The E&P core: from seismic to producing well, mature fields and marginal blocks reopened, frugal data-driven EOR.

Midstream

Logistics & corridor: qualified trucking, hub depots, pipeline integrity — the barrel rolls before the pipe.

Downstream

Refine close by, distribute everywhere: 500–2,000 b/d modules, Tchadium stations and Mobile Stations™ deployed in 24–48 h.

Petrochemicals

Extending the chain: polymers, solvents and EOR inputs formulated from Chadian resources.

GreenTech

Sustainability by design: zero routine flaring targeted, produced water in closed loop, 125 MW solar hybridisation target.

TchadiTech

A digital-native operator: sovereign L1-L4 OT/IT backbone, AI, field robotics, frugal R&D designed in Chad.

Tchaditude — human capital

“Promote local content and grow skills across the energy trades.” Train before extracting: geoscience, drilling, HSE and data tracks, apprenticeships and senior mentoring — 80% Chadian staff targeted. A young nation is an engine for jobs: EnerTchad will act through direct employment and thousands of indirect opportunities across its suppliers.
