Unity · Innovation · Sustainability

Chad exports its crude and imports its fuels. We reverse it.

EnerTchad inverts that equation: an integrated oil company under OHADA law, aiming to produce, refine and distribute in-country — substituting fuel imports, retaining the margin at every link, turning the national resource into affordable energy, jobs and industry. From source rock to pump, the value stays in Chad.

Extract more, spend less, process in Chad.

Clean energy, artificial intelligence and modern enhanced recovery form a virtuous circle: lower costs to unlock more crude, and finance the country's energy autonomy.

From source rock to the pump, an integrated chain.

Exploration-production, transport, modular refining and distribution: the entire value chain built and retained in Chad, driven by our digital foundation.

Access to energy, the first domino of development.

Reliable, affordable energy sets everything else in motion: industry, agriculture, health, education — purchasing power returned to households and a controlled cost of living for Chadians.

Chad's oil, developed by its own children.

Chadian capital, 80 % local content targeted and the Tchaditude academy: train before extracting, for lasting autonomy.

Find your way · Upstream → Midstream → Downstream → Petrochemicals

Four links, four ways in.

Four words come up everywhere on this site — upstream, midstream, downstream (amont, intermédiaire, aval in French) and petrochemicals : these are the four links in the molecule's journey, from reservoir to pump — and on to the bag of fertiliser. Looking for a service, a product or an industrial partner? Come in through the one that concerns you:Exploration-Production finds and produces the crude, Transport & storage moves and stores it, Refining & distribution transforms it and serves it at the pump, and Petrochemicals turns it into fertiliser, methanol and derivatives made in Chad. Four cross-cutting capabilities complete the picture.

Our conviction · Our vision

“From exploration to the pump, we hold every link — the whole chain, in Chadian hands, for the first time from A to Z.”

EnerTchad is all of Chad's energies, brought together.

Those asleep beneath our basins and those alive in our talent: the oil we produce, refine and distribute, the gas we valorise, the sun we capture, and the intelligence of the women and men who put them to work. We gather them into one Chadian hand, around a simple conviction — energy is only worth the good it delivers.

That is why we seekharmony: between the resource and the land that carries it, between growth and the communities that host it, between industrial ambition and respect for living things. From source rock to pump, every link we integrate pursues the same goal — putting energy at the service of Chadians' well-being and the country's development.

A company in formation, we inherit nothing: we are building, step by step, the company that will turn our energies into shared progress.
