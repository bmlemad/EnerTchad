The EOR approach: data first

  • Screening: the right EOR is chosen in the data, not in the catalogue. Every method has its application window — crude gravity, viscosity, depth, temperature, permeability; the reservoir study, then the pilot, are what decide.
  • Sovereign chemistry: natron, gum arabic, cotton cellulose and neem — four Chadian materials qualified for the reservoir: less hard currency leaving the country, a Chadian agricultural supply chain that gets paid, and know-how that stays.
  • Pilot-based method: screening, laboratory, a pilot on 3 to 5 wells of a partner mature field, payment tied to the verified additional barrel above an agreed decline baseline, then extension. First pilot targeted within 2 to 4 years; within that period, phase 1 (years 1-2 after access to a field) includes the pilot.
  • Target fields: the declining mature fields and marginal blocks of the Doba and Bongor basins, subject to agreement with the petroleum rights holder and the State.

Contracting with EnerTchad

Three contract models, one non-negotiable HSE-Q foundation.

  • Per-job or framework services: one intervention, one work order, one price for one-off needs and well emergencies; or rates and SLAs agreed for the year, with mobilisation on demand — wireline, workover, maintenance, logistics.
  • Study & pilot paid per additional barrel: data access under NDA, process screening in your reservoir data, then a pilot on 3 to 5 wells. Payment tied to the verified additional barrel: above an agreed decline baseline, under joint measurement.
  • Consortium or equity stake: local set-up with operators, oilfield-services companies and the academy for tenders and multi-well campaigns; a minority interest when the study demonstrates the potential. Any assignment of interests requires the prior authorisation of the Minister in charge of Hydrocarbons (art. 21, Law No. 006/PR/2007).

The HSE-Q foundation

  • HSE-Q: systematic work permits, LOTO lockout, stop-work authority for every worker, pre-task risk analyses; well barriers and kick control, IWCF/IADC certification targeted for all personnel on pressurised operations. Zero accidents: the HSE objective, from day one.
  • Local content: 80% local content targeted — priority to Chadian companies and talent, inputs sourced in-country whenever possible, skills transfer through Tchaditude.
  • Standards & reporting: ISO 9001 · 14001 · 45001 approach targeted, HSE reporting shared with the client; TRIR, LTIF and Tier 1 and Tier 2 process safety events published from the first worksite, using IOGP definitions.
  • Ethics & compliance: code of conduct, anti-corruption, supplier expectations and the alert channel. EnerTchad never asks for cash, Mobile Money or payment to an “agent”: our only official channels are this site, addresses at @enertchad.com and our official WhatsApp.

The extended E&P offer

Four specialised lines that complete the oilfield-services catalogue — from the laboratory to the control room — and five pre-assembled packs, one per field phase. Details: enertchad.com/amont/services-ep-en

Geosciences & laboratory

Coring & petrophysics, PVT and fluid analyses in Chad (target), reservoir modelling with P10/P50/P90 uncertainties, a phased Field Development Plan (FDP) and a 1P/2P/3P reserves file ready for certification — a marginal-field FDP in 6 to 10 weeks (target).

Advanced interventions

Sand control & gravel pack for unconsolidated sandstones, extended well testing (multi-rate tests, PLT), NDT inspections (ultrasonics, magnetic-particle, radiography), downhole diagnostics by memory slickline.

The digital field

24/7 monitoring from a control centre in N’Djamena (target), SCADA & hybrid gas-solar telemetry, digital twin and production allocation, operator reporting & data room.
