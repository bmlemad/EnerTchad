Vision

Building an integrated energy group with Chadian capital.

The purpose of integration is simple: Chadian consumers should pay much less for their fuels. EnerTchad aims to approach half of today’s official price (700 FCFA per litre of petrol and 800 FCFA for diesel in N’Djamena, the scale in force in 2026) by refining Chadian crude at home rather than importing. It is a target, subject to price approval by the State, carried by a company in formation that serves the national market first and exports nothing as long as the country imports its fuels.

The Company and its vision

Chad · national energy map

Chad, at the heart of the group.

The Central African rift basins, a 1,081 km export pipeline, a single refinery in service: the sector as public sources describe it, and the sites EnerTchad is targeting.

  1. National energy map

    About 1.5 billion barrels of proven reserves, production since 2003 in the Doba basin, and Djermaya, the country’s only refinery (20 kb/d nameplate).

    Sector atlas

  2. Oil basins

    Doba and Bongor in production; Doseo under appraisal; Salamat and Termit, the exploration frontier; Lake Chad, discovered and undeveloped.

    Basins and fields

  3. Logistics corridors

    The Doba–Kribi pipeline (1,081 km, operated by third parties) and the Douala–N’Djamena road corridor (about 1,800 km).

    Transport & Logistics

  4. Industrial hubs

    Djermaya for existing refining; Moussoro, the first targeted mini-refinery, in modular trains of up to 2,000 b/d.

    Refining & petrochemicals

  5. Regional projects

    Doba: mature fields and enhanced recovery; Barh El Gazel: Moussoro; N’Djamena, Moundou, Sarh and Abéché: distribution network.

    Target map

Indicative markers. The sector (reserves, fields, pipeline, Djermaya) belongs to third-party operators, according to public sources; EnerTchad’s sites are targets — company in formation, no asset in operation to date.
