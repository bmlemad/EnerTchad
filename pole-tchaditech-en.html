The conviction · Technology

A digitally native operator does not have to modernise: it starts modern.

From sensor to decision-maker, a sovereign OT/IT foundation (L1-L4), digital twins and frugal R&D conceived in Chad — data becomes one more barrel.

The digital foundation — and the R&D laboratory

TchadiTech is not just infrastructure: it is EnerTchad’s laboratory. Its frugal R&D, conceived in Chad, feeds the seven other poles — enhanced oil recovery with local inputs, modular mini-refinery, field robotics and sovereign engineering. Every innovation is designed for the Chadian context: lean, replicable, operated in-house.
