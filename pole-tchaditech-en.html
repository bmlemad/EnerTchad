Use cases · Technology

From the real problem to the EnerTchad response

Five concrete situations from Chad’s oil sector, and how EnerTchad intends to answer them — as targets, at the pace of the stages.

  1. Case 01

    Challenge

    Deciding blind

    Operations lack real-time visibility in the field.

    EnerTchad response

    Sovereign L1-L4 architecture, digital twins of the reservoir, SCADA.

    Target

    Deciding on data, not blind

  2. Case 02

    Challenge

    Unplanned shutdowns

    Breakdowns and unplanned shutdowns weigh on production and costs.

    EnerTchad response

    Predictive AI + field robotics: drones, pigs, crawlers.

    Target

    Predictive maintenance · fewer shutdowns

  3. Case 03

    Challenge

    Exposed industrial systems

    PLCs and OT networks are vulnerable to threats.

    EnerTchad response

    OT/IT cybersecurity by design, strict L1-L4 segmentation.

    Target

    Continuity and integrity of operations

  4. Case 04

    Challenge

    Scarce digital skills

    Data and OT talent is scarce locally to operate the tools.

    EnerTchad response

    In-house upskilling and digital twins accessible to the teams.

    Target

    Chadian teams self-reliant on data

  5. Case 05

    Challenge

    Slow and risky inspection

    Inspecting tanks, pipelines and flare stacks exposes teams to danger.

    EnerTchad response

    Drones, instrumented pigs and crawlers — inspection without exposing people.

    Target

    Integrity verified · safety strengthened

  • Containerised mobile stationTchadiTech R&D is designing the Mobile Station units (mobilisation time to be confirmed; 5,000 to 15,000 L, LPG, SCADA supervision) — detailed in the Distribution network section. Modular, frugal, redeployable design.
