Technologies · Advanced innovations · Solution

An operator born in 2026, with no technical debt.

EnerTchad drags no legacy system behind it: its technology backbone is carried by TchadiTech, our advanced-technology pole, which brings together OT (operational technologies and industrial automation), IT (information systems and cybersecurity) and AI (data, models, digital twins). We build directly on the best technologies of the moment and stay one step ahead of those that will define the next decade. Modernity is not a marketing claim: it is our starting point.

World-class OT solutions, sized for our ambitions. We will work with the world experts in operational technology and industrial automation — Honeywell, Siemens and their peers — to build SCADA, instrumentation and OT cybersecurity on proven standards, sized for our terrain: isolated sites, autonomous power, satellite links, frugal maintenance.

Robotics · Fit for our realities

The robot goes where humans should not — and covers Chad’s distances.

No showroom humanoids: frugal, off-the-shelf robotics, chosen for three missions — enter dangerous spaces, repeat inspections without fatigue, see the invisible (thermal, ultrasound). Piloted by Chadian tele-operators trained by Tchaditude: robotics multiplies our teams, it does not replace them.

Corridor drones

Pipeline route and site surveillance: thermal camera for leaks, optical for intrusions — kilometres covered per flight, where a patrol costs a full day.

Pipeline · security · thermal
Smart pigs (ILI)

Robots inside the pipe: internal inspection of corrosion and cracks without stopping transport — the API 1160 standard applied to the corridor.

Integrity · API 1160
Confined-space crawlers

Inspection of storage tanks and vessels without human entry: thickness ultrasound, HD imagery — H₂S risk and oxygen-poor atmospheres stay outside.

HSE · zero human entry
Automated EOR injection

Robotised dosing skids for enhanced-recovery chemistry: constant precision 24/7, the natural extension of AI-driven mini-refineries.

EOR · continuous dosing
Target architecture · Four layers

From the valve to the decision-maker — one data flow, auditable end to end.

L1 · EDGE
Acquisition & control

Sensors and controllers on isolated sites (OPC-UA, Modbus, MQTT): local computing, 30-day buffer memory, tolerance to network outages — designed for the Chadian field.

L2 · DATA
Ingestion & lakehouse

Contract-based flows into an in-country data warehouse: raw → refined → decision zones, traceability and audit “time-travel” from measurement to decision.

L3 · AI
Models & inference

Predictive maintenance, yield optimisation, anomaly detection — the models that drive mini-refineries and the network 24/7, with registry and drift monitoring (MLOps).

L4 · SECURITY
OT zero trust

Segmentation, mTLS and internal PKI, industrial intrusion detection, 24/7 SOC backed by a certified partner — IEC 62443 targeted, with the global OT partners.

Connectivity for isolated sites:Redundant VSATPrivate on-site 4G/5GSatellite linksOutage-tolerant edge— because in Chad, the network must never be the excuse.

Reference architecture of the TchadiTech pole — target specification, deployed with the first assets. Cyber references targeted: ISO/IEC 27001 · IEC 62443 · ISO 22301 (continuity) · NIST CSF 2.0. Design rules: MFA everywhere, TLS 1.3 / OT mTLS, secret rotation ≤ 90 days, responsible-disclosure channel.

Data governance & management · Controlled by design

Data: a national asset to protect.

From the valve to the decision-maker, EnerTchad aims for data that is unique, traceable and controlled: collected in the field, hosted and processed in Chad, secured end to end. Operational data remains a Chadian asset, not an exported resource. (target framework · company in formation)
