# Public website readiness across languages

Arabic organization descriptions now reuse the formation-stage wording already published on the Arabic homepage. Legal names and identifiers remain intact. Arabic investor contact opens the enquiry form with Investment selected, and its two French document links disclose their language.

The contact context helper supports the five existing Arabic request types and preserves selection across French, English and Arabic links. The Arabic form reuses its visible heading as its accessible name, accepts fragment focus, and has the same scroll offset and focus indication as the French and English forms. Unsupported Arabic categories remain unselected rather than being assigned an unrelated enquiry type.

Five lightweight newsletter and contact confirmation pages now offer keyboard skip links. The UI checker accepts one-character accessible names, including the existing X social link.

Release validation includes a production build, formation descriptions, public documents, search destinations, public internal navigation, redirects and language pairs. The navigation check excludes historical pages replaced by redirects. It reports glossary and configurator destinations separately because their state is generated by JavaScript; their page availability is checked, while the entire interactive state space is not established by static checks. The standard browser #top target is valid without an element id.

Manual browser checks cover Arabic investment preselection, form focus, language URLs, 390px layout without horizontal overflow, and a generated English glossary term. No enquiry or subscription was submitted.

Business-dependent work remains: approved governance biographies and mandates, dated project evidence, approved financing information, reviewed document translations, and a controlled test of real message delivery. The release does not establish operational results or investment suitability.
