Framework · Before the first barrel

Safety, quality and compliance before the first barrel.

At EnerTchad, safety, quality and compliance are not options: they are the foundations of bankability. The organisation, controls and recognised standards (OHADA, SPE-PRMS, ISO, API) exist from formation — the framework funders and co-investors demand before the first barrel, so that growth is never improvised. Here are the four commitments that run through all our trades.

Our climate ambition. Operations low-carbon by design: routine flaring avoided, site solar and minimal carbon intensity targeted — so that valorising Chadian oil serves development without mortgaging the future. Dated targets of a company in formation, not results.

01 · Health · Safety · Environment

HSE

An approach ALARP (“as low as reasonably practicable”) applicable to every operation, and environmental protection anchored in Chad's realities — the water of Lake Chad and the Sahel, the soils, the air.

The stop-work right
Anyone can stop an operation deemed dangerous: production never comes before people.
An end to routine flaring targeted
Valorising associated gas rather than burning it; cutting emissions at the source.
Water & zero spills targeted
Treatment and valorisation of produced water, reinjection, a zero-discharge objective.
Approach ALARPISO 14001 certification targetedISO 45001 certification targetedGRI · IPIECA targeted

Health

Protecting every worker's health.

  • Occupational medicine and exposure monitoring (targeted).
  • Industrial hygiene: noise, dust, the heat of the Sahel.
  • Fatigue prevention and rotation management.

Safety

Production never comes before people.

  • Process safety: barriers and equipment integrity.
  • Work permits and lockout (LOTO).
  • Risk analysis before every task (JSA) + stop-work right.

Environment

A minimal footprint, anchored in Chad.

  • Water: treatment, reinjection — a zero-spill objective.
  • Air: an end to routine flaring targeted, emissions controlled.
  • Soils & biodiversity: a reduced footprint, site rehabilitation.

Double materiality

An issue is a priority if it weighs both ways: on the company's value, and on the world around it. That is the reading of recent standards (CSRD / ESRS), and the one EnerTchad uses to arbitrate its E-S-G priorities.

Financial materiality
What weighs on value: water access in the Sahel, continuity of the Doba ↔ Kribi corridor, regulated ARSAT prices, site and people safety, the social licence to operate.

The grievance mechanism

A grievance that gets lost is a risk that grows. The target mechanism is simple, dated and traceable — and substitutes for neither the courts nor the whistleblower channel.

1 · Receive
A channel accessible without mandatory writing: a visit, a phone call, a community relay. Every grievance is recorded.
2 · Acknowledge
A dated acknowledgement, with a named contact — the person knows their word has entered the system.
3 · Investigate
Fact analysis, a site visit if needed, a reasoned decision — within a timeframe announced at acknowledgement.
4 · Answer & trace
An explained answer, corrective measures tracked, a consolidated register reviewed by management — without reprisals, ever.
Aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals (UN): SDG 7 — affordable, clean energy · SDG 8 — decent work and growth · SDG 13 — climate action · SDG 16 — effective, transparent institutions.
