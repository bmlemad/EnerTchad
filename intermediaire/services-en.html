In practice

Integrity, five disciplines day in, day out

Moving the flow is one thing; guaranteeing it moves through healthy facilities is another. Five disciplines take care of that, day after day.

Discipline 1

Inspect

Regulatory checks, visual and instrumented inspection of tanks, lines and pumps: every piece of equipment has its file and its schedule — nothing ages unseen.

Discipline 2

Watch the corrosion

Witness coupons, thickness measurements, inhibitor injection: corrosion never stops, and neither does its monitoring.

Discipline 3

Maintain before the breakdown

Planned preventive maintenance on the critical components — valves, pumps, instrumentation: the scheduled maintenance hour always costs less than the unplanned shutdown.

Discipline 4

Prepare for the worst

Spill drills, tested emergency plans, first-response kits in position: the black scenario is rehearsed dry, regularly.

Discipline 5

Count right

Metrology at the interface meters: reconciled material balances, investigated discrepancies — integrity also means the integrity of the numbers.
