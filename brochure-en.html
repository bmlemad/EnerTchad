◆ The model in brief — the margin drivers

Four mechanisms make the EnerTchad model robust, cycle after cycle — margin depends neither on one client, nor one stage of the chain, nor the price of the barrel.

Recurring revenueEnerClub™ & NRJ+™, Tchadium LPG deposit scheme, fleet & institution framework contracts: the customer returns, the contract lasts.
The integration marginOne barrel, five margins — extraction, transport, refining, distribution, petrochemicals — consolidated instead of leaking to intermediaries.
Resilient to cyclesLow crude: downstream breathes. High crude: the EOR barrel outperforms. Services follow activity, not price.
Nothing is lostWater-to-Value™, sulphur, associated gas: costs turned into revenue — and asset-light TchadiTech, EnerConseils, Tchaditude smooths the cycle.
