The conviction · Advisory

You only operate well what you measure well.

Atlas of the Chadian sector, reference data and project de-risking: knowledge of the subsurface and of the market at the service of those who operate — industry and the State alike.

The Atlas at the service of those who operate

EnerConseils turns an independent reading of the sector — geology, basins, reserves, 2025 cadastre, legal regime — into concrete advice: project de-risking, structuring of investment cases and support for operators and the State. Data as a product.

Use cases · Advisory

From the real problem to the EnerTchad response

Five concrete situations in the Chadian oil sector, and how EnerTchad intends to answer them — as targets, in step with the stages.

Case 01
Challenge

Poorly characterised block

Investing in a poorly known block means betting on the subsurface.

EnerTchad response

Sector atlas: basins, cadastre, re-interpretation of the data.

TargetDe-risk before committing capital
Case 02
Challenge

Public decisions without a model

The State arbitrates without a tariff model or consolidated data.

EnerTchad response

Advice to operators and to the State, economic models for the sector.

TargetDecisions informed by data
Case 03
Challenge

Opaque export tariff

The cost of the export corridor remains poorly optimised.

EnerTchad response

Tariff modelling of the Doba–Kribi corridor and throughput scenarios.

TargetOptimised tariff · transparency
Case 04
Challenge

EITI/OHADA compliance to be structured

Operating demands a solid framework of transparency and compliance.

EnerTchad response

EITI support, OHADA law and reporting — compliance through the system.

TargetDocumented compliance · funder confidence
Case 05
Challenge

Local content hard to measure

The real Chadian share of a project is often poorly quantified.

EnerTchad response

Methods for measuring local content and quantified improvement plans.

TargetLocal content measured and managed
Case 06
Challenge

Reserves to be certified

An investor or a lender wants verified figures — not merely declared ones.

EnerTchad response

Audits & independent valuation: reserves certification, integrity, due diligence.

TargetFigures that stand up, sourced and dated
