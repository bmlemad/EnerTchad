The integrated chain

Four links, one molecule.

In the order of the barrel: extract it, move it, refine it, transform it. Each stage funds the next.

  1. I

    Exploration & Production

    Extract more from every field.

    Enhanced recovery on mature fields, marginal-block revival and an integrated oilfield-services force: nine competence domains, with Artificial Lift as the flagship expertise.

    +8–17%additional oil in place targeted

    Discover Exploration & Production

  2. II

    Transport & storage

    Value only counts if it moves.

    Export corridor, distributed strategic reserve and asset integrity: the link that ties the well to the country, from custody metering to the depot.

    1,070 kmDoba–Kribi corridor

    Discover Transport & storage

  3. III

    Refining & distribution

    Keep the value of crude in the country.

    Modular mini-refinery, a station network and distribution to the last mile: fuels, LPG and derivatives, at ARSAT prices.

    2,000 b/dper refining train

    Discover Refining & distribution

  4. IV

    Petrochemicals

    Transform the molecule in Chad.

    Methanol, urea and bitumen: a complex designed for the country’s agriculture, construction and industry.

    6 unitsin 3 phases

    Discover Petrochemicals
