Privacy

Privacy policy

Data protection · GDPR spirit & Chadian framework

This site is static: it collects no personal data automatically, uses no audience-analytics tool and embeds no third-party tracker (advertising, social media, profiling).

Data we process

The only personal data processed is what you send us voluntarily, by e-mail (contact@enertchad.td) or messaging, when you contact us: name, contact details and the content of your message.

Purposes

Answering your enquiry (information, partnership, supply, application, investment) and following up the exchange. No use for unsolicited prospecting, no resale of data.

Retention

Exchanges are kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry, then archived or deleted in line with applicable legal obligations.

Your rights

You have rights of access, rectification, objection and deletion of your data. To exercise them, write to contact@enertchad.td.

Hosting & technical logs

The static-pages host may keep technical logs (IP addresses, timestamps) for security and operations, under its own responsibility as a provider. EnerTchad does not use these logs for profiling.

Contact

For any question about the protection of your data: contact@enertchad.td.
