02 · Industry & B2B buyers

Professional fuel supply

A proposed supply offer for construction, transport, agro-industry and backup power. Recurring requirements in serviceable areas will guide the first commercial phase.

  • First discuss diesel requirements; other products depend on confirmed sourcing, specifications and site needs.
  • Provide the delivery location, monthly volume, seasonality, receiving facilities and purchasing schedule.
  • An offer specifies product, volume, delivery window, quality documents and payment terms. A framework agreement does not itself constitute an order.
  • Supply sources, storage sites and delivery capacity remain subject to confirmation. Commercial operations have not opened.

03 · Hauliers & fleets · B2B

EnerPro · fleet management

A planned service to control vehicle spending and simplify consumption reporting. Its rollout will follow the effective supply and acceptance network.

  • Define vehicle permissions, spending limits, consumption records and invoice reconciliation.

04 · E&P operators & the oil industry · B2B

Engineering & technical services

Two planned technical paths, each with an agreed scope, deliverables and identified project responsibility.

  • Downstream engineering: requirements studies, storage and private-station design, metering, maintenance and consumption monitoring.
  • Upstream services: discuss geosciences, integrity, operations or EOR as separate specialist assignments.
  • Each proposal must identify the expertise available, partners confirmed for the assignment and capabilities still in development.

How it works

From requirements to a recurring relationship

A proposed commercial process adapted to each account; every stage depends on confirmed delivery capability.

  • Qualify the product or technical scope, volume, location, decision-makers, timing and payment requirements.
  • Arrange a technical site assessment when needed, then confirm feasibility and the delivery scope.
  • Prepare an itemised proposal covering pricing terms, responsibilities, acceptance and payment.
  • Agree a paid pilot where appropriate, then review deliveries, invoices and payments before a recurring contract.
  • Review service regularly: complete and timely delivery, product conformity, consumption records and claim resolution.

Opening an account · documents to prepare

What to gather before the first purchase order.

A complete file avoids back-and-forth. Here, from today, is the list of documents a business account will require at commercial opening — the OHADA framework and Chadian downstream regulation leave no room for improvisation.

See the 8 documents to prepare
  • Company identityTrade register (RCCM) and tax identification number (NIF), both valid.
  • Tax standingCertificate of tax compliance, or an equivalent document for public entities and NGOs.
  • Signing authorityMandate or delegation naming the person empowered to bind the company, with identity document.
  • Bank detailsCompany bank details for invoicing and, where applicable, direct debit of card consumption.
  • Delivery sitesAddresses, GPS coordinates and access conditions for each site to be served — tank capacity and offloading height.
  • Forecast volumesAnnual estimate per product: it determines the contractual framework and the delivery schedule.
  • Fleet, if EnerPro cardList of vehicles, registration plates and the limits wanted per card.
  • HSE, if supplierHSE-Q policy, worksite references and professional liability insurance.
No file is being processed to date: EnerTchad is in formation and opens no account, no credit line and no payment channel. This list is published so that you are ready on opening day — and so that no intermediary can claim to speed up a file for a fee. See the anti-fraud notice.

Frequently asked questions

Before writing to us.

How do I get a quote or a framework contract?

Choose your profile above then use your section’s contact button — or write directly to contact@enertchad.com stating volumes, products and location. A single contact answers you and follows you end to end.

Does the ARSAT price really apply everywhere?

That is the network’s target commitment: the ARSAT-approved price, adding nothing to the official scale (N’Djamena prices, plus transport costs in the provinces) — including via Mobile Stations in under-served areas. Company in formation: this commitment will be binding at commercial opening.

When will I be able to order online?

The online shop (fuels, LPG, lubricants, bitumen at the ARSAT price) is presented in preview today; orders will open with the commercial opening. You can already prepare a quote request from the shop.

How do I become a supplier or subcontractor?

A clear HSE-Q specification, structured registration and priority for Chadian local content: go to the Suppliers section above to apply for registration.

What does “company in formation” mean for me?

EnerTchad holds no operating assets to date: everything this page presents — network, lead times, programmes — is a dated target, not a service already open. We prefer to publish verifiable commitments rather than vague promises.
